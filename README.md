# Oil and Gas Production Analysis (2024)

## Project Overview
This project explores offshore oil and gas production records for 2024 using Python. The analysis focuses on production trends, operator performance, well-level production patterns, and relationships between oil, gas, and water production.

## Objectives
- Examine monthly oil, gas, and water production trends.
- Compare production performance across operators.
- Identify the top 10 oil, gas, and water-producing wells.
- Analyse relationships between oil, gas, and water production.
- Calculate and examine the Water-Oil Ratio (WOR).
- Identify unusual production patterns.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Structure
- `data/` – Contains the production dataset.
- `notebooks/` – Contains the Jupyter Notebook with the analysis.
- `visuals/` – For exported charts and visualisations.

## Key Findings
- Production volumes varied throughout 2024, with a noticeable decline in September.
- Shell Offshore Inc. recorded the highest total oil and gas production among the operators analysed.
- BP Exploration & Production Inc. recorded the highest total water production.
- Oil and gas production showed a moderate positive correlation.
- Oil and water production showed a weak positive correlation.
- Some wells recorded unusually high WOR values, particularly where oil production was very low.

## Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates how Python-based data analytics can support production monitoring, operator comparison, well performance evaluation, and the identification of unusual production patterns in the oil and gas industry.

Further analysis using reservoir properties, well characteristics, and longer production histories could provide deeper insight into production performance.
